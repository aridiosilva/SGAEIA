# NIST CSF 2.0 Alignment

- GOVERN: AI/agent policies, owners, supply chain, risk acceptance.
- IDENTIFY: agent/model/tool inventory, crown jewels, risk register.
- PROTECT: NHI, PEP, segmentation, capabilities, secure SDLC.
- DETECT: behavior monitoring, policy denials, drift, anomalies.
- RESPOND: quarantine, revoke, incident playbooks.
- RECOVER: safe restore, model/tool rollback, policy recovery.
